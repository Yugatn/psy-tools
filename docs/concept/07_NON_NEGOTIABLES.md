# Non-Negotiable Rules

**Status: CANON**

1. Preserve accepted canon.
2. Never automatically canonize a proposal.
3. Never silently substitute a conceptual definition.
4. Never silently weaken an ethical constraint.
5. Never silently replace a protected architectural decision.
6. Do not perform destructive cleanup without review.
7. Preserve unresolved ambiguity when it is intentional.
8. Separate detection from interpretation where required.
9. Separate interpretation from intervention where required.
10. Require human confirmation for major conceptual or irreversible architectural decisions.
