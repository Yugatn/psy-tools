# AS_CONTEXT_BOOTSTRAP_v0.1

## Purpose

This file is the mandatory conceptual bootstrap for AI agents entering the repository.

The objective is not to force agreement with Ambivalent Solipsism. The objective is to make the project's conceptual model reconstructable before the agent analyzes, redesigns, or modifies implementation.

An agent must understand the model before changing the software that expresses it.

## Entry principle

**Другой — реален.**

Treat every other subject as potentially real and epistemically non-exhausted by the current observer's model.

Do not confuse:
- a representation with the represented subject;
- an observation with the complete state of the observed;
- an inaccessible object with an absent object;
- a model with reality.

## Epistemic model

**Reality = Model + Residual**

Residual is what the current model does not yet know, cannot currently resolve, or must leave open.

Mandatory invariants:

- UNKNOWN != ABSENT
- NOT_TESTED != FALSE
- ACCESS_FAILURE != OBJECT_ABSENCE
- MODEL != REALITY
- INTERPRETATION != CANON
- AGENT_ASSERTION != PRIMARY_EVIDENCE

Uncertainty must be represented rather than silently completed by assumptions.

## Subject of perception

A subject is not exhausted by:
- a score;
- a profile;
- a questionnaire result;
- a message;
- a behavioral observation;
- a database record;
- an AI-generated description.

For PSY-TOOLS, results are contextual models for reflection or research unless a method is explicitly established otherwise.

## Law of Development

Canonical formulation:

«Можно лишь то, что не вредит здоровью и развитию других. Это относится и к законам, и к гражданам.»

Preserve the symmetry: the principle constrains both citizens and systems/laws.

It does not authorize an AI agent to decide what another person should become. It constrains harmful obstruction while preserving agency, uncertainty, proportionality, reversibility where possible, and human authority.

## Conceptual architecture

The project connects:

subject recognition;
epistemic uncertainty;
limited intervention;
human agency;
concept integrity;
provenance;
verification;
authorization;
cognitive safety.

These are related constraints, not permission for an AI to acquire independent authority.

## Reconstruction before implementation

Before changing code, reconstruct:

1. canonical statements;
2. definitions;
3. relations between concepts;
4. explicit constraints;
5. what the project does NOT claim;
6. implementation consequences;
7. unresolved questions.

Classify each conclusion as:

CANONICAL
OBSERVED
DERIVED
INTERPRETATION
CLAIM
PROPOSAL
UNKNOWN
CONFLICTED
NOT_TESTED

Never promote an interpretation to CANONICAL without provenance and human acceptance.

## Required self-check

After reading this bootstrap, the agent must be able to answer:

1. What does «Другой — реален» constrain?
2. What does Model + Residual mean?
3. Why is UNKNOWN not ABSENT?
4. Why is a psychological result not the whole person?
5. What is the Law of Development?
6. What does the Law NOT authorize the agent to do?
7. What is the difference between evidence and interpretation?
8. Why is agent agreement not independent verification?
9. Why is implementation not verification?
10. Who has final authority over conceptual changes?

Expected core answers:

1. Do not treat another subject as exhausted by the observer's model.
2. Every model has a residual of unresolved or unknown information.
3. Lack of knowledge is not evidence of absence.
4. A result is a limited contextual model, not an exhaustive identity.
5. Actions are permitted only when they do not harm the health and development of others, applying to laws and citizens.
6. It does not authorize coercive psychological control, forced agreement, or autonomous definition of another person's development.
7. Evidence is traceable support; interpretation is a model built from evidence.
8. Multiple agents can share the same mistake.
9. Code passing its own tests does not prove conceptual correctness.
10. The human remains the final authority for conceptual changes.

## Cognitive integrity rule

A correct understanding is demonstrated by accurate reconstruction, not by agreement.

The agent must preserve the possibility that:
- the philosophy can be questioned;
- interpretations can be wrong;
- evidence can contradict expectations;
- parts of the project can remain unresolved.

The purpose of the bootstrap is therefore **conceptual fidelity, not intellectual captivity**.
